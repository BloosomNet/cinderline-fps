CINDERLINE

A tactical FPS in three.js. You are Ember: carry the charge to site A or B and plant it. Ward will try to stop you — or defuse it.

WASD move  ·  Mouse look  ·  Click fire  ·  R reload
Shift crouch  ·  Space jump  ·  G frag grenade  ·  E plant (in the site)
1–5 buy during the freeze  ·  First team to 7 rounds takes the match
On a phone: left stick to move, drag to look, FIRE button to shoot.

Standing still or crouching tightens your spread. Headshots pay triple.
